Privacy Policy

Motivation Elevation · Effective July 24, 2026

The short version: we collect nothing. Motivation Elevation has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and makes no network requests. Everything — your quote history, favorites, and settings — stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

The app keeps your settings (chosen categories, quote cycle times, notification preference), your favorites, and a local history of quotes you've seen (used for the 30-day no-repeat feature). All of this is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It is never transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it all.

What we never collect

No names, emails, or accounts. No location. No contacts or photos. No usage analytics or crash telemetry. No advertising identifiers. No third-party SDKs of any kind.

Purchases

Purchases (lifetime unlock, tips, quote packs) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Purchase receipts are managed by Apple and validated on your device.

Notifications

If you enable the daily notification, it is scheduled locally on your device. No notification servers are involved.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children, and contains no third-party content or links beyond this policy and support page.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future feature requires a network connection), the change will be described here and reflected in the app's App Store listing before it takes effect.
