How the app works

Two fresh quotes a day — one for each quote cycle you set in Settings — with a guarantee that nothing you've seen repeats within 30 days. Tap the widget any time for another quote, right on your home screen.

Common questions

The widget isn't updating

iOS controls widget refresh timing; it usually flips within a few minutes of your cycle time. Tapping the widget refreshes it instantly. If a widget ever appears blank, open the app once, then remove and re-add the widget.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold your home screen until apps jiggle → tap Edit (top left) → Add Widget → search "Motivation Elevation" → pick a size → Add. For the lock screen: touch and hold the lock screen → Customize → Lock Screen → tap the area under the clock → add Motivation Elevation.

I purchased on another device / reinstalled

Settings tab → Support the developer → Restore purchases. Your lifetime unlock is tied to your Apple ID.

How do I change quote times?

Settings tab → Schedule → set your First and Second Quote Cycle to any times you like. Times keep themselves in day order automatically.

Can I suggest a quote category?

Lifetime members get a Request a Category button in Settings — requests directly shape what gets built next.
